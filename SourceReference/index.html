The Wonderful Journey of a Little Water Drop

Meet Dewy and follow one small drop through the water cycle.

Story goal

Turn the pages to see how water moves from the ocean to the sky, back to land, underground, and home again.

Scene 1 · Evaporation

The Sunny Ocean

Dewy rests in the ocean while the warm sun shines above. Heat gives tiny water drops enough energy to rise into the air as water vapour.

Key idea: Evaporation

Water at the surface can change from liquid into invisible water vapour and move upward into the atmosphere.

Scene 2 · Condensation

Cloud Workshop

Higher in the sky, the air is cooler. Dewy and many tiny droplets gather together, forming a soft cloud.

Key idea: Condensation

When water vapour cools, it changes back into tiny liquid droplets. Many droplets together can form a cloud.

Scene 3 · Precipitation

Rain for the Forest

The cloud becomes heavy with water. Soon, Dewy falls as rain, helping the forest, soil and streams below.

Key idea: Precipitation

Water falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail. Here, Dewy returns to the land as rain.

Scene 4 · Infiltration & Collection

Back to the Ocean

Some rain sinks into the soil and becomes groundwater. Other water joins streams and rivers. Together, these paths can carry Dewy back to the ocean.

The cycle continues

The journey ends where another journey begins. Water can return to the ocean and start the cycle again.
